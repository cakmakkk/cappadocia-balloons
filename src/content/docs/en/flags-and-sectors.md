Every balloon flight over Cappadocia depends on the weather, and the weather is judged fresh every morning. The decision comes from the Nevşehir office of Türkiye's civil aviation authority (the DGCA, or SHGM in Turkish). It is published on the public page of the Kapadokya Slot Service Center as a coloured flag for each part of the sky.

## What the flags mean

- **Green flag:** conditions are suitable and take-off is allowed. The final decision then rests with the pilot and the balloon company, so a flight can still be called off on a green morning.
- **Yellow flag:** no take-off. The official page groups yellow with red: take-off approval isn't possible.
- **Red flag:** no take-off. The weather has been judged unsuitable for the hours shown.

## The three flight areas

The main flight area is split into three bands, running from north to south. Each one has its own flag, so on some mornings balloons fly in one area while another stays on the ground.

- **Sector A**, the northern band, around Avanos.
- **Sector B**, the middle band, with Çavuşin, Göreme, Uçhisar, Ortahisar and Ürgüp.
- **Sector C**, the southern band, around Mustafapaşa.

The map on this page is drawn from the coordinates on the official map, and shows the flags as they are right now.

## Secondary regions

Around the main valleys there are smaller secondary regions, numbered 2 to 5 on the official page. Balloon companies can only ask to take off from a secondary region while it is green, and each take-off needs its own confirmation from the Slot Service Center.

## Times are published in UTC

Each flag comes with the time it was set and the window it covers, written in UTC. Türkiye is three hours ahead all year round, so a window listed as 03:25–07:30 runs from 06:25 to 10:30 local time. This site converts every time to Türkiye time for you.

## How this site keeps track

We read the official page every 10 minutes through the morning and once an hour for the rest of the day, and record every change. You can see the result, day by day, on the [history page](../../history/).
