Cappadocia Balloon Status answers one question in plain language: will the balloons fly this morning?

## Where the information comes from

The flags come from the public page of the Kapadokya Slot Service Center, where the daily flight decisions of Türkiye's civil aviation authority are published. The home page reads that page live from your browser when you open it, and every 5 minutes while it stays open. We also check it ourselves several times a day for the history, and every time is shown in Türkiye time.

The outlook for the coming mornings uses weather data from [Open-Meteo.com](https://open-meteo.com/). It is a forecast, not a decision.

## Independent and open

This site doesn't sell balloon rides, and it isn't run by the civil aviation authority, Kapadokya University or any balloon company. Everything we record is public on [GitHub](https://github.com/cakmakkk/cappadocia-balloons/tree/main/data).

## Limits

- A green flag allows take-off, but your pilot and balloon company still decide whether your flight goes ahead.
- If the official page can't be reached, the home page shows the last flags we recorded and when we checked them. Your balloon company always has the latest word.
