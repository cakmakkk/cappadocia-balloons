Balloon flights in Cappadocia start at sunrise, when the air is at its calmest. So the time changes through the year: in Göreme the sun rises at about 05:15 in June and close to 08:00 in early January (Türkiye time).

## When the decision comes out

The flags for each morning are published on the official page shortly before sunrise. In our first records they appeared 35 to 40 minutes before sunrise, and the morning flight window opened about half an hour before sunrise. You can follow it live on the [home page](../../).

## When you'll be picked up

Balloon companies usually collect guests from their hotels about an hour before sunrise. Your company gives you the exact time, often the evening before, so keep your phone close.

## How long it takes

Flights usually last about an hour. With the pickup, waiting for the flags and the landing, set aside the whole early morning.

The table shows sunrise on the 15th of each month. Use it to work out roughly when the flight window opens: in our records it opened about half an hour before sunrise.
