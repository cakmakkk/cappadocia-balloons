On mornings when the flags are green, the sky over Cappadocia fills with balloons at sunrise, and you can watch them for free from the ground.

## Check the flags first

Balloons can only take off where the flag is green. Look at [today's status](../../) before you set off: if sector B is open, the valleys around Göreme, Çavuşin and Uçhisar are the place to be. If every flag is red, the balloons stay on the ground.

## Good places to watch

- **Göreme rooftops and hotel terraces.** Many hotels in Göreme have terraces facing the valleys, and the town sits in the middle of the main flight area.
- **The hills above Göreme.** The viewpoints on the road between Göreme and Uçhisar look out over the valleys where many balloons rise.
- **Love Valley.** Its tall rock pillars are one of the best-known backdrops in the region for balloon photos.
- **Uçhisar.** The highest point in the area gives a wide view across the valleys.
- **Rose and Red valleys near Çavuşin.** Good for a sunrise walk with balloons overhead.

## Tips for an early start

- Be in place about half an hour before sunrise. See [what time the balloons fly](../what-time/) for this month's sunrise.
- Bring a warm layer: mornings on the plateau are cold for much of the year.
- The balloons drift with the wind, so the best spot changes from day to day. A high viewpoint gives you the widest choice.
